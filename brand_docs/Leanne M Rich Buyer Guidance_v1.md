# Leanne M Rich — Buyer Guidance

## Summary of Key Insights

### A. Broke Buyers vs Rich Buyers Messaging

- Broke buyers want fast results, urgency, bonuses, and hype.
- Rich buyers want targeted, micro-specific solutions to their highest value problems.
- Rich buyers value depth, expertise, and identity-based outcomes.

### B. Rich Buyers Want Precision

- They respond to specialized, targeted solutions (e.g., PCOS root-cause hormone support) rather than general promises.
- They prefer clarity and simplicity over long lists of features or bonuses.

### C. Rich Buyers Seek Identity Transformation

- They want to step into the version of themselves they already envision.
- They invest in mentorship to arrive at their desired identity faster.

### D. Content That Reflects Their Psychology Converts Fast

- When messaging mirrors the mindset of a rich buyer, conversion can happen in a single post.
- Messaging should focus on:
  - Hormone-specific outcomes
  - PCOS-related identity shifts
  - Feminine soft-life transformations
  - High-value problems like fertility, energy stability, and long-term health

---

## Key Points (Simplified)

**1. Broke buyers = results-chasers.**

They want fast outcomes ("how quickly can I lose weight?" "how fast can I make money?"). They respond to urgency, discounts, and external motivation.

**2. Rich buyers = identity-driven.**

They pay for precision, not promises. They don't want "weight loss"; they want embodiment — confidence, peace, power, freedom. They already see themselves as someone who invests; they just want a clear, elevated path to get there faster.

**3. They're not sold by bonuses or urgency — they're sold by refinement and alignment.**

The offer needs to feel intentional, curated, and deeply relevant to their lifestyle and values. "Less is more" — quality over quantity, transformation over tactics.

---

## How to Apply This to Your Content

To attract higher-paying women:

- Focus less on "lose belly fat / balance hormones fast" and more on identity elevation: becoming the confident, consistent, feminine version of themselves who finally feels at home in her body and in control of her health.
- Position your program as the refined path for women who are done experimenting and ready for expert-led precision.
- Replace "bonuses" and "free weeks" as the hero — they're add-ons, not selling points.
- Highlight emotional and energetic ROI — peace, confidence, power, structure, freedom — instead of surface-level results.
