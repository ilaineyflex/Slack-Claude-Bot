# SECTION 1: THE CORE PROBLEM THIS GUIDE SOLVES

**THE ROOT ISSUE IN OUR PREVIOUS NAMING, Content and Captions**

Clinical authority and premium positioning are not the same thing. Clinical authority without outcome storytelling produces course names and descriptions that a doctor or another health coach would respect, but that a woman who wants to fit into her jeans again, get her period back, or stop crashing at 2pm will scroll past without a second thought. People do not buy content, mechanisms, or methodologies. They buy the feeling of being in the outfit, having energy to play with their kids, looking in the mirror and recognizing themselves again. Every naming decision must start there.

**THE DUAL OBLIGATION OF N2F NAMING, Content and Captions**

N2F has two audiences inside one buyer: the ambitious, strategic woman who responds to precision and specificity (she wants to know WHY something works) AND the emotionally present woman who responds to outcomes, identity, and dream states (she wants to know WHAT HER LIFE WILL LOOK LIKE when it works). Great N2F naming serves both in the same headline. The name and subheader carry the dream state and the transformation. The description delivers the mechanism. Never swap those jobs.

# SECTION 2: PROVEN NAMING STRATEGIES FROM RESEARCH FOR NAMING

## 2.1 What Research Actually Shows About Winning Course Titles

The following strategies are drawn from published data on course and digital product conversions, including Height Platform's naming research, uteach.io's instructor data, and analysis of top-selling health and wellness courses on Gumroad, Etsy, and Kajabi as of 2025-2026.

### STRATEGY 1: Lead with the transformation, not the topic

The most-purchased health courses on Kajabi and Teachable use a Title: Transformation formula. The title names what you do. The subtitle names what you get. Examples from top sellers in the hormonal health niche:

- Women, Food, and Hormones: A 4-Week Plan to Achieve Hormonal Balance, Lose Weight, and Feel Like Yourself Again (bestselling book that became a course). Note: 'Feel Like Yourself Again' is the emotional anchor that drives clicks.
- PCOS Reset Blueprint: Reset Your Hormones, Reclaim Your Glow (Gumroad seller, \$50 price point, multiple sales). Note: Reset and Reclaim are action verbs that imply the buyer is taking back something that was taken from her.
- Alan Sharp's copywriting course 'Create a Copywriting Portfolio With No Experience' generated strong enrollment because it answered the buyer's most urgent question in the title itself.

The principle: the title does not need to explain the mechanism. It needs to name the outcome or the transformation so clearly that the buyer says 'that is exactly what I need.'

### STRATEGY 2: The 7 to 12 Word Sweet Spot

Research from Heights Platform on 32,000+ courses showed that titles in the 7 to 12 word range outperform both shorter and longer titles in click-through rate. This is because they are specific enough to communicate an outcome but short enough to be scannable on a mobile screen. Our course name plus subheader together should hit this range. The main title should be under 7 words. The subheader handles specificity.

| **Too Short (unclear)** | **Too Long (loses scan-ability)**                                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PCOS Plan**           | The 8-Week Self-Paced Protocol That Addresses Insulin and Cortisol Before Asking Your Body to Lose Fat                        |
| **Hormone Method**      | The Complete Hormone-First Methodology for Women with PCOS and Pre-Diabetes Who Have Not Responded to Conventional Approaches |
| **Belly Reset**         | Restructure Insulin, Cortisol, and Testosterone at the Pattern Level for PCOS Belly Fat Reduction                             |

### STRATEGY 3: Use Numbers When They Carry a Promise

Numbers in course names perform well when they imply speed, specificity, or a clear structure. They work best when the number connects to a tangible outcome, not just a program duration.

- 7 Days to Your First Result: the number implies speed and quick wins, not just a timeline.
- Drop a Size in 8 Weeks: the number implies a measurable, clothing-size outcome.
- 3 Things Keeping You in PCOS Belly Fat Mode: the number implies digestible, actionable content.

Numbers fail when they are generic (8 Week Course) or when the number is attached to a process rather than an outcome (8 Weeks of Protocol Work).

### STRATEGY 4: Power Words That Convert in Health and Wellness

These words are proven to increase click-through in wellness digital product naming, sourced from conversion copywriting research by Unbounce, ConvertFlow, and analysis of top Gumroad health products in 2025:

- RECLAIM: implies the buyer had this thing and it was taken. 'Reclaim your waistline.' 'Reclaim your energy.' Strong because it positions the buyer as someone recovering power, not trying to earn something new.
- FLAT: as in flat tummy, flat belly. Specific body outcome word. Top-performing in PCOS content on TikTok and Instagram. 'Flat tummy' outperforms 'reduced belly fat' in engagement.
- BACK: 'get your body back,' 'your period back,' 'your confidence back.' The word BACK is emotionally loaded because it implies the buyer HAD this thing before. She is not chasing something new. She is returning to herself. This resonates deeply with women who gained weight or lost their period after coming off birth control.
- ACTUALLY: 'what actually works for PCOS.' Signals honesty and implies you are correcting misinformation. High click-through in a niche full of conflicting advice.
- FIT INTO: 'fit into your clothes again.' 'Fit into the dress.' Specific, body-positive, outcome-concrete language that does not require clinical understanding.
- WITHOUT: 'lose the PCOS belly without cutting carbs / without counting calories / without spending hours in the gym.' The without formula directly addresses the objections your buyer has from previous failed attempts.
- FINALLY: implies a problem the buyer has been living with for a long time that is now solvable. Use in descriptions, not course names. Too generic as a standalone title word.
- GLOW: resonates with the beauty and skin-clearing transformation (clearer skin is a major PCOS outcome). Works in subheaders and product descriptions.

### STRATEGY 5: SEO Viability for PCOS Course Names

The following are confirmed high-search terms on Google and TikTok in the PCOS hormonal health niche as of 2024-2026, based on TikTok tag volumes and Google Keyword Planner public data:

- PCOS belly fat: tens of millions of TikTok views. Very high Google search volume.
- PCOS weight loss: extremely high on all platforms.
- How to lose weight with PCOS: consistently top 10 PCOS search query.
- PCOS energy: rising search trend in 2025-2026 as the fatigue conversation grows.
- Hormonal belly fat: rising trend, not PCOS-specific, broader audience reach.
- Lose belly fat PCOS naturally: high purchase intent. 'Naturally' signals the buyer is not looking for medication.
- PCOS bloating: high volume, underserved content.
- Insulin resistance weight loss: rising post-2023 as GLP-1 media coverage created search curiosity.

For N2F course naming, the ideal SEO approach is: use one high-search term naturally in the course title or subheader (PCOS, belly fat, hormonal), then use the proprietary mechanism language (Hormone-First, HormoneSync) in the description where the buyer is already reading and already converting. Do not try to make the proprietary mechanism names SEO-friendly. They are identity and differentiation tools, not search tools.

### STRATEGY 6: The Outcome-First Title Formula

This is the most reliable naming formula for health transformation courses, drawn from analysis of courses generating \$5,000 to \$50,000+ monthly across Kajabi, Teachable, and Gumroad:

**THE FORMULA**

\[What she wants to stop feeling OR what she wants to feel again\] + \[in X days/weeks\] + \[without the thing she dreads doing\]. Example: Finally Fit Into Your Clothes Again in 8 Weeks Without Giving Up the Foods You Love. Or: Lose the PCOS Belly. Get Your Period Back. Feel Like You Again. 8 Weeks.

# SECTION 3: THE ANTI-CLINICAL RULES FOR N2F NAMING, Content and Captions

**WHY CLINICAL LANGUAGE FAILS OUR BUYER**

Our buyer is smart, yes. But smart does not mean she wants to read a textbook to understand what she is buying. She is a nurse, a lawyer, an entrepreneur. In her professional life she reads dense material every day. When she is looking for something to help with her PCOS, she wants RELIEF and CLARITY, not another dense document to decode. Clinical language also signals to her brain 'this is medical, this is complicated, this requires effort to understand.' She has already been failed by the medical system. Clinical language reminds her of that experience. We need to sound like a smart, trusted friend who also happens to know the science, not like a medical journal.

The Elaine brand voice is: bubbly, bold, feminine, warm, direct, and a little bit sassy. Like a knowledgeable Black woman in her early 30s talking to her smart friend over brunch. The clinical version of this brand sounds like a textbook. The real version sounds like this:

The N2F sweet spot is between those two. Specific like the clinical. Warm and direct, slightly dramatic, sassy and funny like the real Elaine. The mechanism is there but it is explained in the language of real life, not a medical paper. Example of the right tone:

**THE N2F TONE IN ACTION**

'your belly isn't holding onto fat because you're not "trying hard enough". you're an action-taker so putting in effort to reach your goal is what you're good at, so your EFFORT was never the problem. your pcos/pmos belly is holding on for dear life because out of balance hormones like your insulin are keeping it there. once we deal with the insulin, that annoying fat starts moving. that's the beauty of a hormone-first approach plan personalized to your specific biology, hormones, goals and schedule.'

That is precise, warm, slightly dramatic, direct, and personal. It explains a mechanism without sounding like a clinic. There is personality, identity mirroring. The premium buyer reads that and thinks: finally someone who actually gets it and isn't talking to me like I'm either a patient or a beginner.


# SECTION 3A: SCENARIO-BASED DESCRIPTION RULE

**WHEN TO USE SCENARIO-BASED DESCRIPTIONS INSTEAD OF DEFICIT LABELS**

This rule applies to all N2F content, captions, course descriptions, and naming. Any time you are tempted to describe a client's situation using a deficit label (overwhelmed, stuck, burned out, struggling, exhausted), replace it with a 2-4 sentence scenario that describes a specific, relatable moment in plain everyday language. The scenario makes the reader feel seen without positioning her as broken.

**THE RULE:** Replace deficit labels with specific relatable moments at a grade 3-5 reading level.

**SCENARIO EXAMPLES (use these as templates):**

*Instead of: "struggling with PCOS weight loss"*

> you've been eating clean for three weeks. the scale hasn't moved. you're doing everything right and your body is acting like it didn't get the memo.

*Instead of: "overwhelmed by conflicting advice"*

> one account says low carb. the next says carbs are fine. your doctor says just lose weight. you've tried three different approaches in six months and none of them felt like they were built for you.

*Instead of: "burned out from trying"*

> you've been consistent. really consistent. and the results still aren't matching the effort. you're not looking for motivation. you're looking for the thing that's actually going to work.

*Instead of: "stuck at the same weight"*

> the number on the scale has been the same for eight months. you've changed your diet twice. you've added workouts. it moves for a week and then comes right back. that's not a willpower problem. that's a hormonal pattern.

**HOW TO APPLY THIS RULE IN AI PROMPTS:**

When prompting Claude or any AI for N2F content, include this instruction: "Do not use deficit labels like struggling, stuck, overwhelmed, burned out, exhausted, or defeated. Replace any situation description with a specific 2-4 sentence scenario that describes what the woman is actually experiencing in everyday life."

# SECTION 3B: NAMING PHILOSOPHY RATIO GUIDE

**DREAM STATE PRIORITY WITH CLINICAL AUTHORITY SUPPORT**

N2F naming uses a context-dependent ratio of dream state language to clinical authority language. The ratio changes based on what the content is doing and who is reading it at that moment.

**CONTEXT-DEPENDENT RATIOS:**

| **Content Type** | **Dream State %** | **Clinical Authority %** | **Rationale** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Course titles and lead magnet names | 80-90% | 10-20% | Buyer is in discovery mode. Dream state triggers the click. Clinical authority supports credibility in the subheader. |
| Rapport/discovery call names | 60-70% | 30-40% | Buyer is warmer. She wants to feel understood AND confident she's talking to an expert. |
| Sales/assessment call names | 50% | 50% | Buyer is evaluating. Equal weight on outcome and expertise. |
| Instagram captions and hooks | 70-80% | 20-30% | Social content must stop the scroll with dream state first. Mechanism is the supporting detail. |
| Course module titles | 80% | 20% | Modules are internal. The buyer has already purchased. Warmth and specificity over clinical credibility. |
| Program descriptions (after the hook) | 40-50% | 50-60% | Once she is reading the description, she wants to understand WHY it works. Mechanism is welcome here. |

**THE PROGRESSION RULE:**

Dream state opens the door. Clinical authority closes it. Never lead with the mechanism in a name, headline, or hook. The mechanism belongs in the description — after the emotional hook has already landed.

**CALL AND FORM NAMING STANDARD (updated May 2026):**

Call names must include at least one outcome or dream state word. Names that are 100% clinical (e.g., "Hormonal Architecture Assessment Call") are not approved for client-facing use. Names that are 100% dream state with no specificity (e.g., "Your Best Life Call") are too generic. The approved range is outcome-first with a clinical anchor in the subheader or descriptor.

*Approved examples:* Hormone Blueprint Call | PCOS Pattern Mapping Session | Belly Fat Root Cause Call

*Requires revision:* Hormonal Architecture Assessment | Phenotype Identification Session | HPA Axis Evaluation Call
